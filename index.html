About IEEE

Who we are and why Ignite exists

IEEE

IEEE is the world’s largest technical professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity.

IEEE Student Branch LBSITW

The student branch encourages engineering students to explore emerging technologies, build projects, and connect with a wider technical community.

Objective of Ignite

Ignite is designed to combine technical knowledge, career readiness, and interactive challenges into a single event that motivates students to learn by doing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who can participate in IEEE Ignite?

Ignite is open to students from all branches who are interested in technology, career growth, and teamwork.

Do I need to register for every event?

You can register for selected events or participate in the full event lineup, depending on availability.

Will participation certificates be provided?

Yes, participation certificates can be issued for attendees based on the event guidelines.

Is there any prior technical experience required?

No prior experience is required for most sessions. The activities are designed to be beginner-friendly.
